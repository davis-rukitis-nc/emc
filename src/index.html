Welcome to Riga

Dear friends at the European Marathon Classics,

I have to be honest — I jumped with excitement when the European Marathon Classics came around. Benchmark marathons, visionary races, and true standouts of the European running scene, all under one roof? It almost sounded too good to be true.

There is just one thing missing: representation for the Baltic region and its crown-jewel event, the Rimi Riga Marathon.

So here I am, writing to you directly to outline why Riga belongs in the European Marathon Classics, in the hope that you’ll consider us joining the series in the near future.

Since 1991 — and under my direct ownership and supervision since 2006 — our team has built a marathon festival that doesn't just mirror Europe's leading races. It brings a fresh, distinct perspective to the European running landscape.

Featuring five World Athletics Label races in a single weekend, combined with one of the continent’s largest Kids’ Days (10,000+ young runners), Riga is the undisputed leader of the Baltic running scene. As the host of the inaugural World Athletics Road Running Championships and an international magnet drawing runners from 120 nations, Riga is a benchmark festival delivering major social, mental, and economic impact for Latvia’s capital and the broader Baltic region.

We believe that no European Marathon Classics circuit would be complete without Riga. Should you consider completing the Classics circle, we would be honoured to be counted in.

Please find our 7 reasons, our concrete offer, and a message from the Mayor of Riga below.

With best regards from Riga, Aigars Nords, CEO & Founder, Rimi Riga Marathon

A message from the city

Dear European Marathon Classics Team,

As Mayor of Riga, I can assure you that our city stands fully behind Aigars Nords and the Rimi Riga Marathon team’s aspiration to join European Marathon Classics. Riga is a vibrant, safe, highly accessible European capital situated securely in the heart of the Baltics, within the EU and NATO.

Every spring, our ancient cobblestones and sweeping Daugava bridges transform into a stage for tens of thousands of runners from more than a hundred nations. Riga offers incredible architecture, warm Baltic hospitality, and a city administration that treats our marathon weekend as our premier annual cultural and sporting asset.

Riga has repeatedly proven its operational muscle hosting major global events — from the IIHF World Hockey Championship and the World Rally Championship (WRC) to the 2023 World Athletics Road Running Championships. Anchored by Riga Airport as Northern Europe’s key regional hub and our national carrier airBaltic, our city infrastructure and hotel bed-night capacity are ready to effortlessly welcome at least 10,000+ European Marathon Classics runners and guests on top of the international community of the Rimi Riga Marathon.

We warmly invite the European Marathon Classics family to join us on May 15–16, 2027, to see our city in full stride and experience the speed, the culture, and the unmatched energy of the Baltic's premier running weekend!
Sincerely, Viesturs Kleinbergs Mayor of Riga, the Capital City of Latvia

Running festival

Punching Way Above Our Weight

We might be a compact European capital, but our influence and international appeal rival Europe's leading races.

With Riga’s population just over 600,000, we don’t rely solely on the classic 42.2K distance. Instead, we’ve engineered the Rimi Riga Marathon as a major, city-wide running festival — a weekend spanning everything from Kids’ Day and our “Inclusivity Race” to the Road Mile and the classic marathon, welcoming a crowd of 46,000+ runners representing 120 nations.

Sceptics might point out that our combined full and half-marathon fields hover around 12,000 runners. But our kids having real athletic fun on the streets, our 10K and 5K runners building towards future marathons, and our massive international footprint make all the difference. International runners make up 60% of our full marathon field and 40% of our half-marathon field, generating an influx of close to 20,000 visiting runners and companions every single year. And last but not least — back in 2018, we were the first ever Northern European–Baltic marathon to earn World Athletics Gold Label!

02

World Champs heritage

Named the “World’s Road Running Capital” by Sebastian Coe

When World Athletics needed a trusted partner to launch the inaugural World Athletics Road Running Championships in 2023, they chose our streets and our team.

The result? Two World Records broken on a single day, leading Sebastian Coe to publicly declare Riga the “World’s Road Running Capital.”

Since then, Riga holds a unique distinction: we are the only marathon weekend globally to host five World Athletics Label distances (Mile, 5K, 10K, Half, and Full Marathon) under one umbrella.

Five labels. One weekend.
In short

We are World Athletics-tested, operationally flawless, and fully equipped to execute complex events at the highest global tier.

03

World-class speed

World-Class Speed (With Room to Grow!)

Riga is flat. Period.

Yes, we route runners over a couple of scenic bridges on our World Championship heritage courses for the fun and sheer atmosphere, but our layout remains flatter than many of the world's standard fast courses.

With targeted investment in elite fields, we are ready to deliver headline-grabbing record times and an endless sea of amateur personal bests. In 2026, our Half Marathon’s Competition Performance Score (9665) was surpassed by only four major events in all of Europe. Our women’s half-marathon record is currently #2 in the world, our men's course record was set by Sebastian Sawe (59:10), and our Road Mile course still holds the World Record set in 2023.

Now, to be completely transparent: our full marathon score for 2026 sits at 8865. Sure, it isn’t at the Major or European Marathon Classics level yet — but we are honest enough to admit it and ambitious enough to scale it alongside the Classics family.

04

Culture impact

A Culture-Driven, City-Wide Running Festival

As an authentic tribute to Latvia’s UNESCO-heritage Song and Dance Festival, our course is lined with hundreds of live choir singers.

This creates an emotional “Singing Marathon” that carries runners across our river bridges on a physical wall of sound found nowhere else on earth.

We also refuse to give out generic race merchandise. Every year, we collaborate directly with the Latvian National Museum of Art and leading contemporary Baltic artists to custom-design our participant shirts and finisher medals. Our current four-year medal cycle spells out “R I G A”, with each letter individually decorated by a legendary Latvian master. “R” was awarded in 2026; runners will return for “I” in 2027.

05

Sustainability

Next-Generation Impact & 100% Carbon Neutrality

Our dedicated Kids’ Day is one of the largest in the world, welcoming over 10,000 children (along with thousands of parents and grandparents) to run and explore 20 different sports completely free of charge the day before the main race.

To protect the future we are building for these kids, we lead by example: we are the only major event in our region to calculate and 100% compensate for our total CO₂e footprint (and we are happy to share our methodology with you).

07

The missing Baltic anchor

The Missing Baltic Anchor (With Real Skin in the Game)

A comprehensive European series needs a strong, strategic anchor in the secure, fast-growing Baltic region.

But we don't expect a seat at the table simply because of our location or past trophies. We believe in earning our place and pulling our weight — our goal with joining the Classics is to double our marathon field to 10,000+ runners within 3 years, while helping to point the international marathon-tourist pipeline towards all the Classics members.

Our offer

Inaugural partnership contribution€50,000

To demonstrate our respect for the decades of investment built by the founding members, the Rimi Riga Marathon is fully prepared to contribute an inaugural partnership fee of €50,000 towards the European Marathon Classics' joint marketing and expansion fund.

We are committed to being an active, hands-on partner by:

  • Cross-promoting member races across our channels.
  • Exchanging EXPO and promotional spaces.
  • Integrating fully into the series' logistical and communication network.
  • Investing directly in joint marketing campaigns.

Furthermore, if the board considers a standalone 42.2K format an essential criterion for membership, we are prepared to restructure the Rimi Riga Marathon to run as a completely standalone marathon distance.

It would be an honour to share the Classics journey with the respected members of the European Marathon Classics. Joining this series aligns perfectly with our vision to stand firmly among Europe’s top 10 most desirable running destinations.

Let’s complete the circle together, shall we?

Prepared by Rimi Riga Marathon team

Although originally not from the elite sports world, they are experienced recreational runners, known for their storytelling and event production skills. In 2006, when the team started their journey with Rimi Riga Marathon, they pretty much had to turn a dreaded activity for many – running – into an appealing social movement, by engaging people, attracting sponsors, involving the City, and persuading thousands of international runners to come and enjoy running in Riga!

Decades later, running is the most popular mass sport in Latvia, while Riga has become the 2023 Road Running Capital of the World, as stated by Sebastian Coe, the president of World Athletics!
